# Couch playback

Language for the sources, programmes and devices involved in watching and listening.

## Language

**Source**:
A media item or ongoing publication available to a Playback. A Source can be
shared without currently playing on any device.

**Playback**:
One programme and the picture/sound outputs following it. Independent Playbacks
have independent state and ordinary-media queues.

**Publisher**:
The device or host service that owns an ongoing shared source. Ending its
publication removes that source from its receivers.

**Receiver**:
A device reproducing a Playback's selected picture or sound.
_Avoid_: Listener when referring to picture and sound together.

**Controller**:
A device from which a person controls permitted playback or outputs. It may
differ from both the Publisher and the Receiver.

**Capture**:
Acquisition of ongoing picture or sound from the publishing device.
_Avoid_: Capture for reading an existing media file or URL.

**Direct playback**:
An ordinary programme on one permitted destination, with its own playback state.

**Relaxed synchronized playback**:
A shared programme whose picture and sound follow a common timeline, with
collaborative programme controls and ordinary buffering.

**Live audio**:
An ongoing audio publication whose audience follows the source and controls its
own listening. It grants no audience control over the programme or its queue.
