# Watch a share together

Status: preview · Scenes: group, coworking

Someone shares a talk or a match from their laptop. The room watches it on the
TV while the sound plays on the speakers around, or several screens in the room
show it. Here everyone must see and hear the same moment, so it's worth being a
little behind the source.

## Journey

1. Share as in [My screen on the TV](my-screen-on-the-tv.md).
2. In **Where it plays**, add a second device's **Picture** or **Sound**.
3. Turn on **In sync**: every screen and speaker follows one clock.

## Done when

- With **In sync**, every receiver shows and plays the same moment.
- With one receiver, Couch plays the share directly, without the delay.
- The sharer's own screen and sound are never delayed.

## Demo

Needs: the HDMI display, a laptop and a phone as a speaker. Content: a video
with speech, shared from a window.

## Known issues

- **In sync** adds a few seconds of delay; it suits watching, not playing.
