# My own thing, then onto the TV

Status: preview

The kids are watching cartoons on the TV. Alessio chooses **Play on** his phone
for a match and watches it on the sofa while the cartoons carry on, their
picture beside the match's at the top of his player. When they finish, he opens
**Where it plays**, presses the TV's name and taps **Move here**: the
match picks up on the TV where it was, with its **Up next**, and his phone
stops.

## Good to know

- **Move here** waits until the match has started playing.
