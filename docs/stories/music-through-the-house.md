# Music through the house, and into the shower

Status: preview

An album plays in the living room. Ester opens **Where it plays** and turns on
the kitchen laptop's **Sound**, and it plays in step. Before her shower she
turns on her phone's **Sound** and takes it into the bathroom. She lowers the
kitchen, and later turns everything down at once with **Volume**, on its own
row above the player's timeline.

## Good to know

- A phone or laptop plays only after it lets others use its speakers
  ([Join a Couch](join-a-couch.md)).
