# Music through the house, and into the shower

Status: preview · Scenes: home

Music plays in the living room. Someone wants it in the kitchen too, in step,
and then takes it into the bathroom on their phone while they shower.

## Journey

1. Play an album or a station.
2. Open the player → **Where it plays**.
3. Turn on **Sound** for the kitchen laptop, then the living-room TV. They play
   in step; no extra setting.
4. In the bathroom, turn on this phone's **Sound** (**Tap to hear** if the
   browser asks).
5. Set each device's level, or the top **Volume** to scale them all together.

## Done when

- Adding a device's Sound brings it in, in step with the others.
- Each device keeps its own level; **Volume** moves them all in proportion.
- Removing a device leaves the others playing.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch computer, a laptop and a phone (the owner's phone rig).
Content: an album or a station.

## Known issues

- On phones, the minimized now-playing bar hides **Where it plays**: open the
  player first.
- Devices must allow their speakers first ([Join a Couch](join-a-couch.md)).
