# Radio while I work, and "what's this song?"

Status: preview · Scenes: personal, coworking

I keep a station on while I work. When a song catches my ear, one tap tells me
what it is, and I find it again later.

## Journey

1. Home → **Radio**: **Built-in** (avoid radio), **Playlists** or **Directory**
   (Radio Browser), narrowed with **Genre** or **Filter stations**.
2. **Play** a station; **Save** it to **Channels & stations**.
3. In the player, **Identify song**.
4. The result, with the station and programme, is in Saved → **Identified
   songs**; a match can be searched across Couch's music sources.

## Done when

- Built-in, playlist and directory stations play, with programme names where the
  station publishes them.
- **Identify song** names the song from a short sample, or says it found none.
- Every identification is kept in Saved, with who asked.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch computer. Content: Lyl Radio or another music station.

## Known issues

- Directory stations get **Identify song** only after they're saved.
- Admins add radio playlists in **Settings → Sources → Radio**.
