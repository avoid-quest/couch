# Radio while I work, and "what's this song?"

Status: preview

Federica keeps Lyl Radio on while she works. A track catches her ear, she taps
**Identify song**, and Couch names it. On Friday she finds it in Saved →
**Identified songs**, with the station and the show, and searches it across
Couch's music.

## Good to know

- Radio playlists are added by an admin in **Settings → Sources → Radio**.
