# My screen on the TV

Status: preview · Scenes: gaming, group, coworking

I play a game, or show a video or photos, from my laptop. It goes to the TV with
its sound, and friends can watch on their laptops too. I keep control and stop
it when I want.

## Journey

1. **Share screen or sound** → **Screen** or **Window**, with **Include system
   audio**.
2. Choose **Play on** → the TV (or no device: it waits in Home's **Shared
   sources**), and the picture's resolution and frame rate.
3. **Start sharing**; the system asks for permission once.
4. Friends **Play** it from Shared sources, or add their screen in **Where it
   plays**.
5. The sharing capsule shows who's watching; **Stop sharing** ends it for
   everyone.

## Done when

- Each viewer plays the picture and sound as soon as they arrive.
- Only the sharing device starts, changes or stops its capture.
- The sharer never sees or hears its own share echoed back.
- 1080p at 60 FPS by default; 720p or original size, and 30, 60 or 120 FPS,
  changeable while sharing.

## Demo

Needs: the HDMI display as the TV and a second laptop. Content: a game or a
video in a window.

## Known issues

- System sound needs macOS 14.2+ or Windows; on Linux a share may go out
  silently.
- Viewers aren't in step with each other; see [Watch a share together](watch-a-share-together.md).
