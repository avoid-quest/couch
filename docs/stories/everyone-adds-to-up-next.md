# Everyone adds to Up next

Status: preview · Scenes: group, home, coworking

At a party, or in the office, everyone queues what they want from their own
phone. Everyone sees the same Up next, who added what, and what plays next.

## Journey

1. **Search or paste a link**: YouTube, Twitch, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, RaiPlay or
   any page yt-dlp understands (**Open as → Webpage / Direct media**).
2. **Play** starts it now, **Queue** adds it to the end, **Play next** after
   what's playing.
3. **Up next**: **Earlier**, **Later**, **Play next**, **Remove** (with
   **Undo**), **Save for later**.
4. When the queue runs out, **Autoplay** carries on with the rest of the album,
   series or playlist.
5. **Save as list** keeps tonight's queue; Saved → **Lists** plays it again.

## Done when

- Every phone on the Playback sees the same Up next, with who added each item.
- Anyone can reorder or remove, and a removal can be undone.
- A saved list plays or queues later, and can be updated from Up next.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch and two phones. Content: a Bandcamp album, a YouTube video and
a pasted link.

## Known issues

- Reordering is by menu (**Earlier** / **Later**), with no dragging.
