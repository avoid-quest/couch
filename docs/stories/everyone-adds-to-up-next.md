# Everyone adds to Up next

Status: preview

At Anna's party everyone has the Couch open on their phone. Teo pastes a
Bandcamp album into **Search or paste a link** and taps **Queue**; Mia finds a
YouTube video and taps **Play next**. Everyone sees the same **Up next** and who
added each song. Someone removes Teo's third album and taps **Undo** when he
protests. When the queue runs out, **Autoplay** carries on with the rest of the
album, and at the end of the night Anna picks **Save as list…** in Up next's
**…** to play it all again next time from Saved → **Lists**.

## Good to know

- To move something in **Up next**, use **Move up** or **Move down**; dragging
  doesn't work.
