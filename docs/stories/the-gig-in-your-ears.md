# The gig in your ears

Status: preview · Scenes: live event

The sound desk's mix goes into a sound card on the Couch computer. The audience
joins with their phones and listens in their headphones, or on the phone
speaker, close to live. The organiser sees who's listening. The same works for
a talk, a silent disco or a listening party.

## Journey

**Organiser**, in the Couch desktop app on the Couch computer:

1. **Share screen or sound** → **Audio input** → **Playback: Live** → **Find
   audio inputs** → the **Device**.
2. Optionally a **Title**, **Now playing** and **Playback buffer floor (ms)**.
3. **Start sharing**. The panel shows **Live** time, **Listeners**, their state,
   **Remove**, **Mute audio input** and **Codec**; native Mac inputs also offer
   **Capture buffer** and **Input channels**.
4. **Stop sharing** ends it for everyone.

**Guest:**

1. Join the Couch, then Home → **Shared sources** → **Join**
   (**Tap to hear** if the phone asks).
2. **Your volume**, mute and **Leave**; the player says **Listening**,
   **Waiting for audio…** or **Reconnecting…**.

## Done when

- Guests hear the input close to live, in step, without controlling the
  programme.
- Each guest's volume is their own; leaving affects only them.
- A dropped phone reconnects to the present, not to a backlog.
- The organiser sees and can remove listeners, and edits the title live.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch computer with an audio interface and phones (the owner's
Behringer and phone rig). Content: a stereo mix.

## Known issues

- Live starts only from the desktop app on the Couch computer.
- No input level meter; lowering the shared delay needs a restart.
- Raising one listener's buffer raises it for everyone.
