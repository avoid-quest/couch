# Make it ours

Status: preview

The flat is tired of one channel's reaction videos, so Irene uses **Hide a
word** and they're gone from everyone's Home and Search. On her own phone she
turns off rows she never uses in Home and makes the **Text size** bigger; her
films play in Italian with **Audio language**. She makes the TV's interface
bigger and taps **Keep**, and turns on **Send error reports** after reading what
it sends.

## Good to know

- A hidden word also hides longer words that contain it, and creators whose
  names do.
