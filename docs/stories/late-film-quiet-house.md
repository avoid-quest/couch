# Late film, quiet house

Status: preview

It's midnight and the house is asleep. Nico plays a film on the TV, opens
**Where it plays**, turns on **Sound** on his phone, the first row as **This
device**, and turns off the TV's, and listens in his headphones. The voices run
a little late, so he presses his phone's name and moves **Sync** to bring them
earlier, and turns on **Compressor** under **Advanced** so the explosions don't
drown out the dialogue.

## Good to know

- Nobody has yet checked by ear that lips match or how the **Compressor**
  sounds.
