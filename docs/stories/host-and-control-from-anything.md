# Host on my computer, control from anything

Status: preview

Pietro installs Couch on the old laptop by the stereo, picks **Host on this
computer** and names it Living room. From the sofa, his phone and Elena's
laptop control it; on the laptop she presses **Space** to pause and holds it to
watch at **2×**. Closing the window leaves Couch playing for everyone, and
**Quit Couch** stops it. At a friend's flat, Elena chooses **Another Couch** and
her laptop joins theirs instead.

## Good to know

- Couch doesn't offer to add itself to a phone's home screen.
- Hosting on a Mac or Windows computer hasn't been checked lately.
