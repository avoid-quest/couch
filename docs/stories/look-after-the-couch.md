# Look after the Couch

Status: preview

Paolo looks after the house's Couch. In **Settings → Devices & network** he sees
every paired device, uses **Make admin** on his partner's phone, and uses
**Forget** on the phone his nephew lost, which loses access at once. When both
admin phones are reset, the Couch computer shows **Make a device the admin**,
and Paolo pairs his new phone, types the **Admin code** and taps **Become
admin**.

## Good to know

- If nobody is an admin and the Couch computer has no screen, getting back in
  means restarting Couch with a recovery code
  ([Administrator access](../usage.md#administrator-access)).
