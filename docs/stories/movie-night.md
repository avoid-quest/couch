# Movie night

Status: preview · Scenes: home, group

Friends on the sofa pick a film or a series together, watch it on the TV and
steer it from their phones. Next week they pick up where they stopped.

## Journey

1. On a phone: **Search or paste a link**, or Home → **Sources** → a catalogue.
2. **Open** a film or series; series show **Seasons** and **Episodes**.
3. **Play on** → the TV, or **Play S1 E2**, **Queue**, **Save** for later.
4. **Remote**: arrows, **OK**, **Back**, transport, volume, and **Type on TV**.
5. Next time: Home → **Continue**, or the series' page → **Resume S1 E3**.

## Done when

- Films, series and anime from the catalogues open, show their episodes and play
  on the TV.
- Any paired phone can drive the TV's Couch like a remote, including typing.
- Stopping partway saves the place; **Resume** starts there on any device, and
  **Remove from Continue** can be undone.

## Demo

Needs: the TV (or the HDMI display) and a phone. Content: a StreamingCommunity
series episode and an AnimeUnity film.
