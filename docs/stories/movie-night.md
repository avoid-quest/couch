# Movie night

Status: preview

On Friday, four friends on the sofa search for a series on their phones, open
it and choose **Play on** the TV. Giorgio uses **Remote** to pause for pizza and
**Type on TV** to search for the next one. A week later Home's **Continue** row,
or the series' **Resume**, picks up at the next episode.

## Good to know

- Some 123MoviesFree titles fail to play when the site is having trouble.
- There's no way to mark something as watched.
