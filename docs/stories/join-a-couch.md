# Join a Couch

Status: preview

Davide arrives and wants to add a song. Chiara opens **Pair a device** on the
Couch; Davide scans the QR code with his phone's camera, names it, taps **Pair**
and he's in, with no app and no account. Later he turns on **Speakers** under
**Let other devices use** so the music can play through his phone too; until
then his phone's row in **Where it plays** offers nothing, and its details say why.

## Good to know

- A phone's speakers and screen start off, and nothing after pairing says so.
- The browser may warn that the connection isn't private. Continue for this
  address only. The warning can come back later.
