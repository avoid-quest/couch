# Join a Couch

Status: preview · Scenes: home, group, coworking, live event

A friend arrives and wants to add a song or watch along. They join from their
phone's browser in under a minute: no app, no account. Their phone stays theirs
until they choose to lend its speakers or screen.

## Journey

1. On the Couch, an admin opens **Settings → Devices & network → Pair a device**:
   an address, a QR code and six digits.
2. The friend scans the QR code. If the browser warns that the connection isn't
   private, they continue for this address only.
3. The code is filled in. They choose **Name this device**, then **Pair**.
4. Later, to let others play through the phone: **Settings → This device →
   General → Let other devices use → Speakers / Screen**.

## Done when

- A phone joins with a QR scan or six digits, in the browser, with no install.
- The first paired device becomes the admin; later ones can browse, play, queue
  and control shared playback, but not change the Couch's settings.
- Nobody, admins included, can use a device's speakers or screen until that
  device allows it, and it can take that back.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch computer and a phone (the owner's phone rig).

## Known issues

- Nothing after pairing tells people that their speakers and screen start off.
- The certificate warning can come back when Couch issues a new certificate.
