# My computer's or turntable's sound on every speaker

Status: preview · Scenes: home, group

The record player, a mixer or a music app on my computer plays through every
speaker in the house, in step, and anyone can still pause or change what plays.

## Journey

1. **Share screen or sound** → **Audio input**, **Playback: Relaxed** and the
   **Device** (or **System audio** for the computer's own sound).
2. **Play on** the speakers, or add them later in **Where it plays**.
3. **Start sharing**; **Stop sharing** ends it everywhere.

## Done when

- The input plays in step on every chosen speaker.
- People keep normal control of the Playback; only the sharer stops the source.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch computer with an audio input, a laptop and a phone. Content: a
record or a music app.

## Known issues

- For an audience following a show, use [The gig in your ears](the-gig-in-your-ears.md)
  instead: Live has its own controls for host and guests.
- System sound needs macOS 14.2+ or Windows.
