# My computer's or turntable's sound on every speaker

Status: preview · Scenes: home, group

The record player, a mixer or a music app on my computer plays through every
speaker in the house, the Couch computer's own included, close to live and in
step.

## Journey

1. **Share screen or sound** → **Audio input** → **Find audio inputs** → the
   **Device** (or **System audio** → **Playback: Live** for the computer's own
   sound).
2. **Play on** the speakers, or add them later in **Where it plays**. The Couch
   computer's own speakers **Join** from Home → **Shared sources**.
3. **Start sharing**; **Stop sharing** ends it everywhere.

## Done when

- The input plays in step on every chosen speaker, the Couch computer's
  included; a speaker that can't keep up plays a step later, on its own.
- There's no Pause or Up next while it plays; only the sharer stops the source.

## Demo

Needs: the Couch computer with an audio input, a laptop and a phone. Content: a
record or a music app.

## Known issues

- For an audience following a show on their phones, see
  [The gig in your ears](the-gig-in-your-ears.md).
- System sound needs macOS 14.2+ or Windows.
