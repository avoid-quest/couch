# My computer's or turntable's sound on every speaker

Status: preview

Matteo plugs the turntable into his laptop's sound card. In **Share screen or
sound** he chooses **Audio input**, picks the card, chooses the living room and
kitchen speakers and taps **Start sharing**. The record plays all through the
house, close to live and in step, until he taps **Stop sharing**.

## Good to know

- Sharing the computer's own sound needs macOS 14.2 or later, or Windows.
