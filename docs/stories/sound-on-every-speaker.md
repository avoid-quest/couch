# My computer's or turntable's sound on every speaker

Status: preview

Matteo plugs the turntable into his laptop's sound card. In **Share screen or
sound** he chooses **Audio input**, picks the card, sees its level move and taps
**Start sharing**, and in his player's **Where it plays**, open while nobody
listens, turns on the living room speakers' **Sound**, then the kitchen's. The record plays all
through the house, close to live and in step, until he taps **Stop** on the
**Sharing** pill.

## Good to know

- Sharing the computer's own sound needs macOS 14.2 or later, or Windows.
