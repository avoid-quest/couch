# Keep everything up to date

Status: preview

On a Sunday morning Marta opens **Settings → Updates** on her phone and taps
**Check for updates**. The Couch, the TV, the laptop in the study and the media
tools are all listed. She reads **What's new**, taps **Update 3 devices**, and
because the kids are watching cartoons, Couch asks before it stops them.

## Good to know

- Phones and browsers aren't listed: they always open the Couch's current
  version.
- **Stable** has no release yet; every app follows **Preview**.
